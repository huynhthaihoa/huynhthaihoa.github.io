State Estimation Simulations
Personal Project · Ongoing since August 2026

From-scratch simulations spanning the recursive-vs-batch spectrum of state estimation on the SO(3)/SE(3) manifolds: IMU integration/pre-integration, point-cloud pose tracking (EKF vs. Invariant EKF vs. batch Gauss-Newton), pose-graph optimization with loop closure, and full bundle adjustment (joint camera-pose + landmark refinement, Umeyama-aligned to ground truth). Every method implemented twice — hand-rolled NumPy Lie-algebra math, and via the manif Lie-theory library — backed by a structured set of theory notes spanning math foundations, the Kalman-filter family, and the factor-graph/smoothing family.
